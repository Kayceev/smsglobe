FAQ

Before you buy

Still unsure? Talk to support.

What is a virtual number?

A real phone number that receives SMS online, with no SIM card. You use it where an app asks for your phone number, and the code it sends shows up in your DeemaxHub account. Your own number stays private.

What if the code doesn't arrive?

You can cancel at any point before a code comes in and get refunded straight away. If you leave it, the number closes after 20 minutes and refunds itself.

Can I get another code on the same number?

Yes, within 20 minutes of buying it. Tap "Request another code" on the order. After that the number is released, so finish your sign-up inside that window.

How do I add money?

From Add Money on your dashboard. Payments go through Flutterwave, in naira, and the balance shows as soon as the payment is confirmed.

Why aren't eSIM and RDP orders instant?

Someone on our team sets each one up with the carrier or the server host. You'll get an email the moment it's ready, and you can follow the status on your Orders page.

Is it legal to use a virtual number?

Yes. Using a second number to protect your privacy is legal. What you do with an account still has to follow the app's own rules and our terms.
